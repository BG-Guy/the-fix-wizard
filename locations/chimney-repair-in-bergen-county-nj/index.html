Our Chimney Repair Services in Bergen County, NJ

The Fix Wizard provides professional chimney repair throughout Bergen County. Whether you're in Hackensack, Paramus, Fort Lee, Englewood, or anywhere else in the county, we bring licensed technicians, transparent pricing, and same-day availability directly to your door. Below is a full breakdown of every chimney repair we handle in Bergen County.

Chimney Sweeping & Cleaning in Bergen County, NJ

Annual chimney sweeping removes creosote buildup — the primary cause of chimney fires — from fireplaces across Bergen County. We use rotary brushes and a HEPA vacuum to clean the flue from top to bottom, leaving no soot or debris in your home.

Chimney Tuckpointing in Bergen County, NJ

Crumbling mortar joints are common in Bergen County's older brick chimneys, especially after freeze-thaw cycles. We grind out the deteriorated mortar and pack new mortar to the correct depth and profile, stopping water infiltration before it damages the flue liner.

Chimney Crown Repair & Sealing in Bergen County, NJ

The chimney crown is the concrete cap that sheds water away from the flue opening. Cracked or spalled crowns in Bergen County allow water to enter the masonry and accelerate deterioration. We repair cracks with crown-specific elastomeric sealant or pour a new crown for severe damage.

Chimney Cap Installation in Bergen County, NJ

A stainless steel chimney cap prevents rain, animals, and debris from entering the flue while allowing exhaust to escape freely. We measure the flue tile dimensions and install the correctly sized cap on your Bergen County chimney with stainless screws.

Chimney Flashing Repair in Bergen County, NJ

Failed step flashing or counterflashing at the roof-chimney junction is one of the most common sources of attic and ceiling water damage in Bergen County homes. We remove the old flashing, install new step and counterflashing, and seal with appropriate roofing caulk.

Firebox Repair in Bergen County, NJ

Cracked or spalled firebrick in the firebox is a fire safety issue — damaged brick can allow combustion gases to reach the framing. We re-mortar loose joints with refractory mortar and replace spalled or cracked firebrick to restore a safe combustion chamber for your Bergen County fireplace.

Chimney Liner Installation in Bergen County, NJ

A properly sized and installed flue liner is required by code in NJ and ensures combustion gases exhaust safely. We install stainless steel liner systems in Bergen County chimneys for oil, gas, and wood appliances — including insulation wrap for proper draft.

Chimney Waterproofing in Bergen County, NJ

Unpainted or unsealed masonry in Bergen County's wet climate absorbs water through the brick face, causing spalling and freeze-thaw damage over time. We apply penetrating, vapor-permeable masonry waterproofer to the entire chimney exterior — blocking water entry while allowing moisture to escape from inside.

Chimney Draft Repair in Bergen County, NJ

Smoke backing into the room when you light a fire signals a draft problem — often a blockage, an undersized flue, or negative house pressure. We diagnose the cause and correct it in Bergen County homes whether the fix is a cap change, damper repair, or air-sealing the attic.

Fireplace Damper Repair in Bergen County, NJ

A stuck or missing fireplace damper wastes energy year-round by allowing conditioned air to escape up the flue. We repair throat dampers or install top-mount dampers on Bergen County fireplaces — sealing the flue when not in use and providing reliable control when firing.

Brick & Mortar Restoration in Bergen County, NJ

Spalling brick faces and deteriorated mortar are common across Bergen County's older housing stock. We clean, tuckpoint, and apply consolidant to stabilize aging masonry — restoring the chimney's structural integrity and preventing further weathering.

Chimney Inspection in Bergen County, NJ

A Level 1 chimney inspection is recommended annually and before each heating season for Bergen County homeowners. We inspect the accessible interior and exterior of the flue and firebox, checking for obstructions, deterioration, and code compliance, and provide a written report.
