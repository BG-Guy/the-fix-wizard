Drywall Hole Repair in Passaic County, NJ

Small or large holes in drywall are one of the most common repair calls we get across Passaic County. Whether it's from a doorknob, an anchor that pulled through, or accidental damage, we patch it cleanly and blend the surrounding texture so the repair is invisible.

Drywall Crack Repair in Passaic County, NJ

Cracks in drywall — whether hairline or wider settlement cracks — are especially common in older Passaic County homes. We reinforce the crack with mesh tape and joint compound, then feather it out and texture-match so it won't reappear.

Water Damage Drywall Repair in Passaic County, NJ

Water-stained or bubbled drywall in Passaic County homes often signals a past or ongoing leak. We assess the moisture source, dry the area, cut out the damaged section, replace it cleanly, and prime to prevent stain bleed-through before finishing.

Drywall Patch & Texture Matching in Passaic County, NJ

Invisible patching requires matching the original texture — orange peel, knockdown, skip trowel, or smooth. We test-spray on a sample area first to dial in the match before applying the final finish across your Passaic County home.

Ceiling Drywall Repair in Passaic County, NJ

Ceiling repairs in Passaic County are trickier than wall repairs — gravity works against you, and sanding overhead creates dust throughout the room. We use the right tools and protect your floors and furniture so the job is as clean as the result.

Drywall Joint & Tape Repair in Passaic County, NJ

Bubbling or cracking tape along seams is a sign that the original installation used too little compound or the tape was applied incorrectly. We re-bed the tape, feather new compound, and finish it correctly so it holds for years.

Drywall Corner Bead Repair in Passaic County, NJ

Dented or cracked corner beads are common in high-traffic areas of Passaic County homes. We replace damaged metal or vinyl bead, apply compound, and sand to a sharp, clean corner that can handle daily contact.

Popcorn Ceiling Removal in Passaic County, NJ

Popcorn ceilings in many Passaic County homes were applied decades ago and are often outdated or damaged. We wet-scrape, smooth, and prime the ceiling — leaving a clean, modern surface ready for paint.

Drywall Skim Coat in Passaic County, NJ

A skim coat gives walls a perfectly smooth, paint-ready surface — ideal for Passaic County homeowners who want to eliminate texture or prep walls after wallpaper removal. We apply two thin coats of compound, sand between coats, and finish to a level-5 surface.

Drywall Mold Damage Repair in Passaic County, NJ

Mold on drywall is common in Passaic County bathrooms, basements, and laundry rooms. We safely remove affected drywall, treat the framing, and install new moisture-resistant board before finishing and painting.

Drywall Installation in Passaic County, NJ

For additions, renovations, or full room replacement, we hang, tape, mud, and finish new drywall in Passaic County homes to a smooth, paint-ready surface. We work clean, protect your floors, and haul away all scrap.
