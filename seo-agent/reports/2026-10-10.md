# SEO weekly report: The Fix Wizard, 2026-09-13 to 2026-10-10

## Summary
Search Console data is still very thin — 2 clicks and 83 impressions over 28 days — so there were no query-driven opportunities to act on this week (no striking-distance pages, no low-CTR top rankings, no content gaps). Instead, this cycle's checklist pass on the five key pages caught a real, user-visible mistake: the homepage and both service hub pages (chimney, handyman) were telling Google and visitors "Serving all of New Jersey" in their meta descriptions, left over from an earlier version of the site. That's wrong and against the site's own rules (CLAUDE.md explicitly limits the service area to Cherry Hill and a specific list of South Jersey/Philadelphia-area towns, and bans any North Jersey references) — fixed on all three pages. Everything else below is informational; nothing needs a decision this week beyond noting the index cleanup already happening on its own.

## Changes in this PR
1. **Homepage** (`/`) — meta description said "Serving all of New Jersey." Replaced with "...in Cherry Hill, NJ & the Philly area..." to match the real service area. File: `sections/head/head.html`.
2. **Chimney services hub** (`/services/chimney/`) — same false "Serving all of New Jersey" claim in the meta description, replaced with "near Cherry Hill, NJ and Philadelphia." File: `services/chimney/index.html`.
3. **Handyman services hub** (`/services/handyman/`) — same fix. File: `services/handyman/index.html`.

None of these were query-driven; they came from running the key-page quality checklist (title/meta/canonical/H1/schema) since there was no actionable query data this week. All three are listed as key pages in the site config. Ran `node build.js` afterward and confirmed the new copy made it into `docs/`; `git diff --stat` shows only the three source files and their regenerated `docs/` output changed.
