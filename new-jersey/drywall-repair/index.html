Every Repair We Handle

No hole too small, no damage too stubborn. Serving Bergen, Essex, Middlesex, Monmouth, Somerset and every county across New Jersey.

Hole Patching

Doorknob holes, anchor blowouts, and accidental damage repaired flush with the wall surface.

Crack Repair

Hairline cracks, settlement cracks, and tape seam splits repaired and reinforced to prevent return.

Water Damage

Stained, bubbling, or soft drywall from leaks cut out, replaced, and blended back perfectly.

Texture Matching

Orange peel, knockdown, skip trowel, popcorn, or smooth — we match your existing finish exactly.

Paint Touch-Ups

Final paint blending so the patch disappears into the wall — no visible lines or color gaps.

Corner Bead Repair

Dented or cracked corner beads re-set and mudded so sharp, clean corners are restored.

Nail Pop & Screw Fixes

Protruding fasteners reset, dimpled, and skimmed — common in homes that have settled.

Ceiling Repair

Sagging sections, cracks along joists, and water rings on ceilings repaired and refinished.
