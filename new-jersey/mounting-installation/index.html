Mounting & Installation

Every Job We Handle

From a single floating shelf in Bergen County to a full gallery wall in Monmouth — we mount it straight, secure, and finished across all of NJ.

TV Mounting

Any TV, any wall. Flat, tilting, and full-motion mounts installed into studs for maximum hold and clean positioning.

Above-Fireplace Mounting

Safe, properly angled mounts over fireplaces — with cable management routed down through the wall or mantel.

Cable Management

In-wall routing hides cables completely; surface raceways offer a clean look without cutting drywall.

Floating Shelves

Single shelves or full gallery walls — level, anchored into studs, and rated for the load you need.

Curtain Rods & Blinds

Rods and blind brackets hung level and at the right height — inside or outside mount, any window size.

Mirrors & Artwork

Heavy mirrors, gallery walls, and large prints hung safely with the right anchors and hardware for the wall type.

Soundbars & Speakers

Soundbars wall-mounted under your TV, bookshelf speakers positioned and wired cleanly along walls or ceilings.

Bathroom Hardware

Towel bars, toilet paper holders, robe hooks, and grab bars anchored solidly into tile or drywall.

How It Works

Simple as 1–2–3

Tell us what needs to go up. We show up with the right mounts, find studs, and hang it right the first time.

1

Tell Us What to Hang

Share the TV size, shelf dimensions, or what you need hung. We confirm the right mount or hardware so we arrive prepared.

2
