Chimney & Masonry Questions

What New Jersey homeowners ask most before booking chimney and masonry work.

If you use your fireplace regularly or notice any changes in draft, odor, or visible exterior cracking, call for an inspection immediately regardless of when the last one was.

Tuckpointing is the process of removing deteriorated mortar from joints and replacing it with fresh mortar. If the mortar between your bricks is recessed more than ¼ inch, crumbling, or missing entirely, your chimney needs tuckpointing. In New Jersey, most chimneys older than 20 years need at least some repointing — water and freeze-thaw cycles degrade mortar joints steadily over time.

It depends entirely on where the cracks are and how severe. Exterior cosmetic cracks in the brick face are less urgent than cracks in the crown, flue liner, or mortar joints. We always recommend a professional assessment before using a fireplace with any visible cracking — especially in older New Jersey homes where the liner condition can't be assessed without a camera inspection.
