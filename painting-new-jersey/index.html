Q&A

Painting Questions

What New Jersey homeowners ask most before booking a painting job.

A single room typically takes 4–6 hours including prep, prime, and two coats. A full-floor repaint runs 1–3 days depending on size and prep needs. We give you a realistic timeline upfront — we never rush coats to finish faster, especially in NJ's humid seasons when dry time is extended.

We move light furniture and protect all flooring with drop cloths. For very large or heavy pieces, we ask that you clear those in advance or let us know so we can plan accordingly. We never paint around furniture — we always protect it or move it.

We primarily work with Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams — both perform well in New Jersey's climate and offer the durability our quality standards require. We can work with a specific brand or sheen if you have a preference. We never use budget-tier paints on our jobs regardless of cost pressure.
