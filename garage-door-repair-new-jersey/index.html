Garage Door Repair

Every Job We Handle

From a snapped spring in Bergen County to a dead opener in Ocean County — we cover every garage door problem across New Jersey.

Spring Replacement

Broken torsion and extension springs replaced safely — the most common and dangerous garage door repair in NJ homes.

Cable Repair

Frayed, snapped, or off-drum cables replaced and tensioned correctly so the door travels straight and safe.

Opener Repair & Install

Belt-drive, chain-drive, and direct-drive openers repaired or replaced — with remotes and keypads programmed before we leave.

Off-Track Realignment

A door that's jumped its tracks is a safety hazard. We re-seat rollers, straighten tracks, and test the full travel before finishing.

Panel Repair & Replacement

Dented or damaged panels repaired or replaced — matching your existing door style and finish wherever possible.

Rollers & Hinges

Worn nylon or steel rollers and loose hinges replaced — grinding and shaking are almost always caused by these small parts.

Weatherstripping

Bottom seals and side weatherstripping replaced to keep out NJ wind, rain, and cold — and to close the energy gap under the door.

Smart Opener Install

Wi-Fi enabled openers installed and configured — open and monitor your garage from anywhere in New Jersey or beyond.
