Chimney & Masonry

Every Job We Handle

NJ's freeze-thaw cycles are the harshest test of any masonry. We repair what NJ winters break — across Morris, Bergen, Essex, Monmouth and every county.

Tuckpointing

Failing mortar joints ground out and replaced — the most important preventive maintenance for any NJ chimney or brick structure.

Chimney Crown Repair

Cracked or crumbling crowns repaired or replaced — the crown is your chimney's first line of defense against NJ rainfall and freeze-thaw damage.

Spalling Brick Repair

Flaking, cracked, and spalling bricks replaced with matched materials — restoring structural integrity and appearance.

Flashing & Waterproofing

Failed flashing at the roofline repaired or replaced, and masonry sealed with penetrating waterproofer to block NJ moisture intrusion.

Chimney Cap Install

Stainless steel chimney caps installed to keep rain, snow, birds, and debris out of the flue — extending the life of your entire chimney system.

Full Chimney Rebuild

When repairs aren't enough, we rebuild from the roofline up — matching original brick and mortar color for a seamless result.

Exterior Masonry

Retaining walls, steps, patios, and exterior brick features repaired and restored throughout New Jersey properties.

Efflorescence & Sealing

White mineral staining treated at the source and masonry sealed to prevent recurrence — a common issue in NJ's wet climate.

If you use your fireplace regularly or notice any changes in draft, odor, or visible exterior cracking, call for an inspection immediately regardless of when the last one was.

Tuckpointing is the process of removing deteriorated mortar from joints and replacing it with fresh mortar. If the mortar between your bricks is recessed more than ¼ inch, crumbling, or missing entirely, your chimney needs tuckpointing. In New Jersey, most chimneys older than 20 years need at least some repointing — water and freeze-thaw cycles degrade mortar joints steadily over time.
